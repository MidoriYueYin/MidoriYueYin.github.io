Narua (also known as Na or Mosuo) is a Sino-Tibetan language with a rich tonal system. This project builds on summer fieldwork, combining corpus analysis of phonotactic patterns with wug-test style experiments on tone sandhi to ask what speakers productively know about their tone system.

**Status:** data collected; analysis in progress.
